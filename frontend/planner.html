How ChronoGrid Works

A deterministic, verifiable campus optimization engine paired with human approval.

STEP 01
AI Interprets
Faculty and students describe room bookings or schedule adjustments in plain English or Hinglish without learning complex code.
STEP 02
Python Validates
Deterministic rule validation verifies all 14 hard constraints: room capacity, equipment, lunch hour, cohort overlaps, and faculty leaves.
STEP 03
CP-SAT Solves
Google OR-Tools minimal perturbation engine generates conflict-free proposals while preserving as many original assignments as possible.
STEP 04
People Approve
Single source of truth governance: Dean Academics, Department Coordinators, and Venue Incharges review diffs and activate revisions.
